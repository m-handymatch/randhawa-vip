Privacy

What we keep, and why.

What we collect

  • Your account: email address, password (stored scrambled by our sign-in provider), first name and ZIP code.
  • Your choices: the nearby cities you pick.
  • What you post: your page (name.localconnect.site), posts, replies and reports. Pages, posts and replies are public.

How it's used

  • Your ZIP and nearby cities choose which news, events, weather and posts you see.
  • Your email is used only to sign you in and for account emails (like confirming your address). We don't sell or share it, and there are no ads.
  • Your page shows your city, never your ZIP or email.

Services we rely on

  • Supabase (accounts and data), Cloudflare (hosting).
  • Your browser loads public information directly from Wikipedia/Wikimedia, the National Weather Service and Open-Meteo, and fonts from Google Fonts. Those services see your IP address like any website you visit.

Your choices

  • You can delete your posts and replies anytime, and edit your page.
  • To delete your account and everything in it, email the site owner from the address you signed up with.
