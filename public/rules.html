House rules

Short version: be a good neighbor.

Be kind and be real

  • Treat neighbors with respect. No harassment, threats, hate or personal attacks.
  • Use your real first name and post as yourself. No pretending to be someone else.
  • Keep it local and useful: news, questions, recommendations, lost and found, events, things for sale or free.

Keep people safe

  • Don't post anyone's home address, phone number or other private details, including your own, in public posts.
  • No illegal content, no sharing things you don't have the right to share, and nothing sexual.
  • Report emergencies to 911, not here.

No spam or selling services in bulk

  • One post per item or question. Posting is limited to 10 posts and 60 replies a day.
  • Businesses may answer recommendation requests, but no repeated ads.

Pages and what happens if something goes wrong

  • Your page (yourname.localconnect.site) and your posts are public: anyone with the link can read them.
  • Use the Report button on any post or reply. Anything reported by three neighbors is hidden until a moderator reviews it.
  • Moderators may hide posts, pages or accounts that break these rules.
  • You can delete your own posts and replies anytime.
